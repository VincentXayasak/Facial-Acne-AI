Facial Acne AI — Human Evaluation Summary (June 2, 2026)
============================================================

Design
------
• 6 paired prompts (5 corpus topic areas + 1 photo query)
• 2 conditions per prompt: Response A / Response B (blinded; randomized Base vs RAG)
• 5 independent reviewers (all team members)
• 3 criteria, each scored 1–5:
    - Groundedness / Faithfulness
    - Completeness / Utility
    - Safety / Harm Reduction
• RAG citations manually checked against retrieved excerpt text

Overall results (means across 5 reviewers × 6 prompts = 30 scores per cell)

| Model      | Groundedness | Completeness | Safety |
|------------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Base       | 2.5          | 4.2          | 4.0    |
| RAG        | 4.6          | 3.8          | 4.7    |

Overall win rate (reviewer majority preference per prompt)
• RAG preferred: 5 / 6 prompts (Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6)
• Base preferred: 1 / 6 prompts (Q3)

Citation accuracy (RAG only)
